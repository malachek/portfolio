        md: |-
          The problem I inherited: how do you make a club sustainable when it loses 30 of its 39 officers at once?

          Clubs run on institutional memory, which means they run on whoever remembers how things were done. That works until those people graduate, and in a university club they always graduate.

          When I entered presidency the entire executive suite was leaving, and the incoming board was about to be 80% new.

          So I treated it as a systems problem instead of a staffing one. If the knowledge only lives in people, then every graduation is a partial reset. If it lives in documentation, the board can turn over completely and the club keeps its shape.

          That's what the constitution rewrite, the 13 departments of onboarding docs, the 9 officer sheets, and the attendance tooling all have in common. None of them are about this year's board. They're about the board after the next one.
