# Do producer / PM postings at the target studios look at portfolios?

Research for the production.malachek.com decision (brief §5). Done 2026-09-26 from public postings and hiring guides.

## Short answer

Rarely. Of about 10 producer, associate producer and PM postings readable at the target studios, **one** mentions a portfolio: Crystal Dynamics' entry-level Associate Producer role, where it is *preferred* ("a portfolio showcasing any personal or academic game development projects"). The rest screen on production experience, shipped titles, tools (JIRA, Favro, Airtable) and team size.

**Recommendation:** skip a full producer site. Build **one production case-study page** (VGDC plus Burnt Out Games releases) that shows real process artifacts: a schedule or sprint board, a scope-cut or risk decision, a postmortem, team sizes and outcomes. Link it from production-track resumes. Keep shipped credits prominent on the resume itself, since that's what these postings screen for.

## Industry guidance

- [Analysis of ~70 producer postings](https://romanluks.eu/blog/analysis-what-are-the-job-requirements-for-a-game-producer/): "shipped titles" appears 22 times; portfolios are not explicitly mentioned.
- [GameDevProducer.com](https://gamedevproducer.com/posts/portfolio-tips-for-aspiring-game-producers/): producers should show process evidence ("Show me a sprint board. Show me a post-mortem you wrote…"), framed as case studies, not screenshots.

## Gaps

- No readable producer posting for HoYoverse, and no actual Epic producer posting.
- The Nintendo and Roblox postings were for adjacent roles.
